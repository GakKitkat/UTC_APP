## 2026-10-09 - Added accessibility attributes to SVG icons\n**Learning:** Inline SVGs in Laravel's default welcome page lacked proper ARIA attributes, causing screen readers to misinterpret decorative vs informative graphics.\n**Action:** Always ensure inline `<svg>` elements have either `aria-hidden="true"` (if decorative) or an `aria-label` and `role="img"` (if informative) for proper screen reader flow.
