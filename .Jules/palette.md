## 2024-05-21 - Decorative SVGs and Accessibility
**Learning:** Using `aria-hidden="true"` on decorative SVGs placed alongside descriptive text links significantly reduces screen reader noise and improves the navigation experience. Additionally, using `role="img"` and `aria-label` on informative SVGs (like logos) is crucial for screen reader users to understand the image content.
**Action:** Always add `aria-hidden="true"` to decorative icons and `aria-label` to informative SVGs lacking text context.
